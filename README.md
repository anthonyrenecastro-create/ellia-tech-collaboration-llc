# Axiom Zeta Innovations LLC — Corporate Site

Production corporate site for **Axiom Zeta Innovations LLC**, served at
**https://axiomzetainnovations.com**.

Written in plain English on purpose: anyone should be able to read the page
and understand what each thing does.

## What we make

The page opens with a featured section for **AI Change Radar**
(https://aichangeradar.com, available now) — a service that watches the AI
vendors a business depends on (25 and counting) for product, pricing,
security, and policy changes, and sends one short brief every Monday: what
changed, who it affects, and the next step. Radar has its own site; this
page simply points to it.

Below that, this company's own systems:

1. **Sentinel** (coming soon) — a smoke alarm for business AI. It watches
   every answer a company's AI chatbot or AI search gives, learns what good
   answers look like, and flags answers that start to drift — with a clear
   action for each one: let it through, steady it, retry, switch to a
   backup, or hand it to a person. Tiers: Solo $59.99/mo, Teams $89.99/mo,
   Sovereign $119.99/mo (early preview).
2. **Cryptocurrency Augury Prism (CAP)** (available now, $49.99/mo) — a
   crypto market tool. It follows live Coinbase prices, blockchain flows,
   market mood, and economic news, weighs seven different checks against
   each other, and writes briefings in plain English. It tests itself
   against real past prices and shows the results, good and bad. Not
   financial advice.
3. **Syzygy Planetary Manifold Telemetry** (working prototype) — a weather
   forecast for computer networks. It keeps two pictures of a network —
   what is happening now and what is likely to happen next — and flags the
   moment they stop matching, so problems get caught early.
4. **Quadra Kestrel** (research system) — AI that runs on the device
   itself instead of in a data center, powered by the Quadra-Matrix
   brain-inspired learning system. First test case: self-flying drones
   that must react instantly, with no internet connection.
