# Axiom Zeta Innovations LLC — Corporate Site

Production corporate site for **Axiom Zeta Innovations LLC**, served at
**https://axiomzetainnovations.com**.

Written in plain English on purpose: anyone should be able to read the page
and understand what each thing does.

## What the site covers

Four research tracks, plus the mission and contact:

1. **Harmonium Research Track** — SetSilk Bio-C smart-skin materials for
   energy devices (10 cm × 10 cm sensing patch, 16–64 sensing regions).
   Phase 1 planned; TeraOpenScience Science Challenge entry.
2. **Multi Modal Recursive Multiverse Research Track (MMURM)** — an
   N-parameterized derivation layer for multiverse structure. Admissible
   N ∈ {5, 12}; no branch pinned down. Describe-only; no public repo link.
3. **Phase Recursion Ontology Research Track** — the foundational framework,
   published as a monograph by Anthony Rene Castro (v1.0, August 2026,
   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22046543); book available at
   transcendentrecursion.online/edition/phase ($6.99).
4. **QuadraSeer Research Track** — sovereign AI that runs on the device:
   Quadra-Seer Sovereign (free download, github.com/axiomzeta),
   Quadra-M.I.N.D. Sight, and Quadra Kestrel edge AI research.
