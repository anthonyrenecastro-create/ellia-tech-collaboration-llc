Planetary AI Telemetry Engine

HRM 4.0 Planetary
Manifold Telemetry

Working prototype

A dual-world-model telemetry workbench built on the Helical Recursive Manifold: one world reconstructs what is happening across the network, the other simulates what could happen next — and scores the divergence between them in real time.

Dual-world counterfactual twin

Model A reconstructs ground-truth observational state from packet-delayed telemetry; Model B projects a +500 ms speculative trajectory — forecasting buffer bloat, thermal throttling, and routing shifts before they land. Continuous divergence scoring flags when the future stops matching the plan.

Non-Euclidean state-space propagation

State advances on a helical manifold: graph-Laplacian diffusion coupled with Duffing-Landau cubic reaction dynamics, stabilized by projection operators and bounded by admissible physical limits. The math is the product — not a dashboard skin over one.

Conservation of Explanation ledger

Every state transition carries an attribution ledger — each delta must account for itself across event, correction, and transport terms. If the books don't balance within tolerance, the step doesn't ship. Inspectable, auditable telemetry.

Server-side Gemini synthesis

Gemini models synthesize telemetry narratives server-side: the engine computes, the model explains. A planetary canvas visualizes the carrier complex while inspectors drill into nodes, events, and ledger entries.
